Invariants and gauges — one line of the congruence, three rulers

Section along one ray. Drag the two slits, the glide station and the probe. The cross-ratio along the line is the same in every ruler; where each ruler puts the plane at infinity is the gauge.

What to look for. In band 1 every colored tick is a level mark — a place where one ruler's coordinate reads j/8. Reach ticks are equally spaced in z because Reach pins ∞ at ∞ (affine). Cross-slit and Turn ticks crowd toward the right because each pins ∞ at a finite mark (Möbius). All three are three-pin coordinates on the same line, so in band 2 the cross-ratio of any four depths reads the same on every ruler — that is the invariant. What differs is the green diamond: the image of the plane at infinity. Reach sends it off the end; Turn puts it at 1 by fiat; the cross-slit puts it at 1 − Z₂/Z₁, wherever the slits happen to be — the physical rig has no say.

The two pushbroom limits are the two glide gauges. Press F₂ far out: the ∞-pin walks onto the plane at infinity, the cross-slit ticks straighten into equal steps and the diamond leaves through the right end — Reach. Press F₁ far out: the 1-pin walks onto the plane at infinity, the diamond settles at 1, and x(z) becomes z/(z + |Z₂|) — Turn, with the surviving slit as the station (ledger row 5 measures the match live). So the pairing F₁ ↔ plane-at-∞, F₂ ↔ station is what the rig itself does in the limit. Fuse the slits is the pinhole: F₁ = F₂, the formula reads 1 everywhere and the cross-slit ruler collapses.

Names. The cross-slit's pins come out of the standard convention as S → 1, F₂ → 0, F₁ → ∞. Band 3 lets you apply any element of S₃ to the names; the value of P moves by the same Möbius map, and the ledger checks it. Press ω² from standard to put the surface at 0 (the document's table). Once the surface sits at 0, only one element besides the identity keeps it there — third, x ↦ x/(x−1) — and it swaps the names of F₁ and F₂. That single switch is the whole of the pairing question.

Gauge. Band 4 is the kernel: with the surface unpinned, k slides P along the ray and drags the surface mark S to 1 − k while F₁ and F₂ stay put — the image is unchanged, the depth is not. Pin the surface and k is forced to 1. Band 5 is the transport from the six-step check: one cross-channel inversion, product 1.

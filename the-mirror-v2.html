what this shows

The gold circle is the anchor in cross-section: centered at the principal point, radius R = √(Z₁Z₂), the geometric mean of the two slit depths. The duality δ acts on depth as reflection in this mirror: the probe P at depth z and its image P† at Z₁Z₂/z are inverse points — the tangent from the outer one touches the circle directly above the inner one, the classical witness of z·z† = R². This is VM·VI = VP² promoted to the anchor of the whole engine: the camera reading and the glide reading of one depth are each other's mirror images, and the mirror flips depth reciprocally — near for far.

The mirror also swaps the channels, because it swaps the slits: the crossing arrows carry νᵤ to νᵥ† and νᵥ to νᵤ†, product 1 always. Drag the slits together and the crossing collapses: on the seam Z₁ = Z₂ the swap becomes invisible, the same-channel product closes too, and the configuration is its own mirror image — fused means self-dual. The circle's crossing of the axis at +R is the fixed depth: the one place the mirror moves nothing.

F and A. Both are on the stage, and both sit at the principal point. The depth axis is the ghost normal — the depth line through the principal point — so its footprint F is the principal point, z = 0. A is the image of depth ∞ along the same line, and the mirror sends ∞ ↔ 0: the far end of the axis lands on the principal point as A, and F is sent to the far end. Press Send P toward ∞ and watch P† close on A; press Send P toward F and P† runs off toward ∞. The fixed points ±R flank the swap. A footprint off the axis sits on a parallel depth line (the F′ toggle); the transport law there is the same z† = Z₁Z₂/z, so the witness circle is carried to F′ unchanged — the sphere's own section through that line is smaller, which is why the six-step check reads the transport through the axis section and not the sphere's.

A note on the letter. The quantity in the swap strip is ν, the Greek nu, not the letter v: νᵤ = z/Z₁ and νᵥ = z/Z₂ are depth measured in units of each slit's own depth. Channel u is the crimson slit, channel v the blue one.
